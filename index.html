FinGraphAI

AML detection that thinks in networks, not rows.

Money laundering hides in relationships — circular flows, mule rings, layered transfers. FinGraphAI models accounts and transactions as a property graph, runs Cypher traversals to surface the patterns, then scores each account on what it triggers. Built for Hack2Future (LTIMindtree × Microsoft) as a top 100 finalist.

      05 About

      Engineer by trade, curious by default.

      I'm Naveen, a software engineer in Hyderabad. I work at LTIMindtree, where the day is usually split between Java and Spring Boot on one side and a React screen on the other — the kind of work where the hard part isn't the code, it's working out what the data is actually trying to tell you.

      I like graphs more than most people do. Half my favourite problems are really relationship problems wearing a spreadsheet costume: who paid whom, what loops back, where the pattern repeats. FinGraphAI came out of that obsession.

      Outside work I build things for the pleasure of finishing them — a receipt parser because my inbox was a mess, a brick breaker because it was the first thing I ever made. I'm happiest when a problem is slightly bigger than the time available, and stubbornly solvable.

      01
